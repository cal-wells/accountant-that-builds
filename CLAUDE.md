## Design Context

This is a **brand** surface (personal portfolio - design IS the product). Before any design or UI work, read `PRODUCT.md` (strategy, voice, anti-references) and `DESIGN.md` (visual system: tokens, typography, components, do's & don'ts).

**Brand in one line:** "the accountant that builds" - credible, modern, approachable; fintech-clean, not scary-finance. One cobalt accent (`#2563eb`), Bricolage Grotesque headings + Inter body, true off-white canvas (`#fafafa`). Avoid: the generic AI-template look, navy-and-gold corporate finance, developer-experiment / jargon vibes, and clutter.

## Source material: the brain vault

This site's job is to *prove* AI and coding fluency to a non-technical finance reader, not
claim it. The evidence already exists in written form at `~/projects/brain`, a compiled
knowledge vault. When writing copy about how Callum actually works with agents, read it
rather than inventing a plausible-sounding version.

Start at `~/projects/brain/personal/index.md` - it is the map, one line per page. The pages
most likely to be relevant here:

- `personal/wiki/llm-wiki-pattern.md` - the knowledge-vault design he runs, and where it
  deliberately deviates from the published pattern
- `personal/wiki/agent-review-loop.md` - how work is split between agents and the human
- `personal/wiki/system-review-pass.md` - the review method, and how it differs from the
  software workflow it was adapted from

**Read-only, and cite rather than copy.** These are compiled pages with `confidence:` grades
and sources; a claim lifted out of one and flattened into marketing copy loses the
qualification that made it honest. If a page says `medium`, the site does not get to say it
with certainty. Where a page marks a `> **Conflict:**` or `> **Unverified:**`, that claim is
not portfolio material.

**Never read, quote, or derive anything from `~/projects/brain/career/`.** It is a separate
local-only repository, deliberately has no remote, and its contents do not enter this repo -
which has one. This applies even when the task is obviously about CV or positioning copy;
that is precisely when the mistake is most tempting. If you think you need something from
it, stop and ask Callum.
