# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Finance hiring managers and FP&A leads, often non-technical, doing due diligence on a shortlisted candidate. They typically arrive from a link in a PDF CV, skim quickly, and want fast, tangible proof that the claimed AI and coding skills are real and applicable to finance, without needing technical literacy to judge it.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Callum Wells, "the accountant that builds" - a Group FP&A Manager and ICAS Chartered Accountant. It exists to prove, not just claim, comfort with AI and code: the site itself is the evidence. Success is the portfolio converting a hiring manager's interest into an interview, supported by the strategy metrics (application-to-interview conversion, inbound contact, time on site, positive mentions in interviews).

## Brand Personality

Credible, modern, approachable. The voice is confident but warm and plain-spoken, translating technical capability into finance-native outcomes (faster reporting, sharper forecasting, real efficiency) rather than tooling jargon. The feeling a hiring manager should leave with: "this person is the real deal, and easy to work with."

## Anti-references

- The generic AI-template aesthetic (the default AI-generated look).
- Scary or stuffy traditional corporate finance (navy-and-gold, big-bank / Big-4 formality).
- A developer-experiment / terminal-flavoured / jargon-heavy "built by an engineer" vibe that loses a non-technical reader.
- Cluttered, busy layouts with too much competing for attention.

## Design Principles

- **Proof over claims.** The artifact is the evidence. Show, don't tell.
- **Finance-native translation.** Frame AI and code in the business outcomes a finance leader recognises, never in tooling jargon.
- **Scannable in under a minute.** Respect a busy hiring manager's time; hierarchy does the work.
- **Approachable credibility.** Professional without being intimidating - fintech-clean, not scary-finance.
- **Restraint with a point of view.** Clean and uncluttered, but distinctly Callum, not generic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (scroll reveals and page transitions disable when set). Visible keyboard-focus indicators on all interactive elements. Body and accent text maintain AA contrast on the off-white background.
